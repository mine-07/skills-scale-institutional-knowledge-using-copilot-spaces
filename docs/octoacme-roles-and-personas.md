# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate the Agile delivery process, remove impediments, and coach teams on best practices. They serve the team, not as a manager, but as a process enabler and blocker remover.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and escalate team blockers and impediments
- Coach the team on Agile and OctoAcme processes
- Monitor sprint health and team velocity
- Help resolve conflicts and improve team dynamics
- Remove distractions and enable focus on delivery

### Goals
- Enable the team to self-organize and deliver consistently
- Maintain sprint rhythm and predictability
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement and psychological safety

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups with the team
- One-on-one coaching conversations
- Sprint ceremonies facilitation
- Blocker escalation to PMs and stakeholders

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers:** Removes blockers, facilitates collaboration, protects focus time
- **Project Managers:** Escalates risks and dependencies, provides velocity data
- **Product Managers:** Supports sprint planning and backlog refinement

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers ensure that OctoAcme products are usable, accessible, and aligned with customer needs. They lead user research, design, and validation activities throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and interviews to understand customer pain points
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Perform usability testing and gather user feedback
- Collaborate with developers on implementation details
- Ensure accessibility and design consistency across products
- Advocate for user needs in prioritization and planning discussions

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, user-centered products
- Reduce post-launch usability issues
- Maximize customer satisfaction and adoption

### Typical Communication
- Design artifacts (wireframes, prototypes, design specs)
- User research reports and insights
- Usability test findings and recommendations
- Design review sessions with developers and product managers

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers:** Validates customer needs, incorporates research into prioritization
- **Developers:** Collaborates on feasibility and implementation
- **Project Managers:** Provides design timeline and dependencies

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define the overall technical strategy, design patterns, and standards for OctoAcme projects. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with long-term technical vision.

### Responsibilities
- Design solution architectures for complex problems
- Establish and communicate technical standards and patterns
- Identify and manage technical debt
- Conduct architecture reviews and provide technical guidance
- Evaluate new technologies and integration approaches
- Work with teams to ensure scalability and performance

### Goals
- Ensure technical decisions support long-term product goals
- Reduce maintenance burden and technical debt
- Enable teams to build scalable, resilient solutions
- Foster consistent technical practices across projects

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and architecture diagrams
- Architecture review sessions with developers
- Technical standards and guidelines documentation
- Long-term roadmap and technical strategy updates

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers:** Provides architectural guidance and reviews code designs
- **Project Managers:** Communicates technical risks and dependencies
- **Product Managers:** Advises on technical feasibility and trade-offs

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business needs and technical delivery. They gather requirements, analyze processes, and ensure that solutions address stakeholder needs effectively.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct stakeholder interviews and requirements gathering
- Document business processes and user workflows
- Create detailed requirements specifications and acceptance criteria
- Support Product Managers in backlog refinement and prioritization
- Validate that delivered solutions meet business requirements
- Identify process improvement opportunities

### Goals
- Ensure delivered solutions solve real business problems
- Reduce rework due to misalignment or unclear requirements
- Improve stakeholder satisfaction and adoption

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and specifications
- Process flow diagrams and use cases
- Stakeholder interview summaries
- Backlog refinement sessions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers:** Supports backlog refinement and prioritization
- **Developers:** Clarifies requirements and acceptance criteria
- **Project Managers:** Identifies risks and dependencies related to requirements
- **Stakeholders:** Gathers and validates business needs

---

## Support Lead

### Role Summary
Support Leads manage post-release operations, triage issues, and ensure continuous feedback flows back to the product team. They are the voice of the customer during project execution.

### Responsibilities
- Triage and escalate production incidents and bugs
- Manage user support queue and response times
- Collect and synthesize customer feedback
- Identify patterns in support requests and escalate to Product
- Collaborate on post-release communication and release notes
- Support go-live activities and user onboarding

### Goals
- Minimize time-to-resolution for critical issues
- Improve customer satisfaction and retention
- Provide early signals of product issues or gaps
- Enable continuous improvement through customer feedback

### Typical Communication
- Incident reports and escalations
- Customer feedback summaries and trends
- Post-release status updates
- Support metrics and dashboard reporting

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **QA/Testing:** Collaborates on issue triage and testing of fixes
- **Project Managers:** Escalates blockers and risks from support queue
- **Developers:** Partners on root cause analysis and fixes
- **Product Managers:** Reports on customer feedback and support trends

---

## Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Stakeholders are business leaders, sponsors, and other representatives who have interest in project outcomes. They provide guidance, approvals, and resources.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic guidance
- Approve major decisions and trade-offs
- Ensure alignment with organizational goals
- Support communications to broader organization
- Remove organizational barriers

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver strategic value
- Maintain alignment with business priorities
- Enable quick decision-making when needed

### Typical Communication
- Monthly status updates and dashboards
- Milestone approvals and decisions
- Escalation and incident communications

---

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When assigning responsibilities or communication patterns, reference the appropriate persona to maintain consistency.
